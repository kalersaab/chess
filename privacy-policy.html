Privacy Policy for Chess Engine

Last Updated: September 5, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Chess Engine ("we", "our", or "the app") handles user information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

TL;DR: We don't collect ANY personal data. Everything stays on your device. No ads, no tracking, no data collection.

Information We Collect

Information We DO NOT Collect

Chess Engine is designed with privacy in mind. We do NOT collect, store, or transmit any of the following:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • User accounts or profiles
  • Game history or statistics
  • Analytics or usage data
  • Any other personally identifiable information

Information Stored Locally

The app stores the following data locally on your device only:

  1. Game State: Current board position, move history, and game settings
  2. User Preferences:
    • Board color theme selection
    • Clock settings (time control preferences)
    • Difficulty level preferences

This information:

  • Remains on your device
  • Is never transmitted to external servers
  • Is not accessible to us or any third parties
  • Can be cleared by uninstalling the app

How We Use Information

Since we don't collect any personal information, there is no data to use, share, or sell.

The locally stored preferences are used solely to:

  • Remember your board color preference between sessions
  • Maintain game state when you navigate within the app
  • Provide a personalized experience

Third-Party Services

Chess Engine does NOT integrate with any third-party services, including:

  • Analytics platforms
  • Advertising networks
  • Social media platforms
  • Cloud storage services
  • Payment processors

The app operates entirely offline and does not require an internet connection.

Data Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties because we do not collect any data.

Children's Privacy

Chess Engine does not collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The app is safe for users of all ages.

Data Security

All data stored by the app remains on your device and is protected by your device's security measures. We recommend:

  • Keeping your device's operating system updated
  • Using device security features (passcode, biometric authentication)
  • Being cautious about device access

Your Rights

Since we don't collect any personal information, there is no data to:

  • Access
  • Modify
  • Delete
  • Export
  • Object to processing

However, you can always:

  • Clear app data through your device settings
  • Uninstall the app to remove all local data
